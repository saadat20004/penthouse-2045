The space

A penthouse that feels like your own.

High above the Flatiron District, Penthouse 2045 pairs pale tiled surfaces and dark metal framing with built in seating and big windows that flood the room with daylight. Step outside to the rooftop terrace and the city skyline becomes your backdrop for drinks and dancing.

  • Whole venue, all yoursNo sharing the floor with other events. You book the full penthouse.
